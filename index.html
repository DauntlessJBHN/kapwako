Our Purpose

About the Initiative

Rooted in the Filipino value of Kapwa—recognizing ourselves in others.

Why "Kapwa Ko"?

Kapwa Ko: Gift-Giving Activity for the PDLs of Batangas City Jail is a grassroots donation drive dedicated to supporting Persons Deprived of Liberty (PDLs) in Batangas City. Many PDLs face limited access to basic hygiene supplies, clothing, and emotional encouragement from the outside community.

Through this initiative, we aim to remind every individual that they are not forgotten—that compassion transcends walls and restorative kindness builds a better community for all.
