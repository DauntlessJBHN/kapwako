Sharing the Warmth of Kapwa This Christmas

Christmas is a season of hope, grace, and togetherness—reminding our PDLs that they are remembered and loved.

Why We Celebrate With Them

Kapwa Ko: Gift-Giving Activity for the PDLs of Batangas City Jail is a Christmas outreach initiative dedicated to bringing comfort and festive cheer to Persons Deprived of Liberty (PDLs). For many inside, the holidays can feel isolating without basic personal care items or a special holiday meal.

Through your generosity, we are assembling Christmas care bundles filled with everyday essentials and holiday treats—proving that the true spirit of Christmas shines brightest when shared with those who need it most.
